# Off Register workshop

The workshop now ships with its treatment in [VICE Substances](vice-substances.md). The following guide describes the retained 0.2.10 workshop content. It implements the starter manual loop from [the gameplay design](lsd-gameplay-design.md), with drawing and distinct minigames on the physical equipment and lab-oven-style cabinet loading and unloading. It requires the beta / IL2CPP game, MelonLoader 0.7.3 and S1API (Forked by Bars) 3.2.1-beta.7. The integrated visual treatment retains the 0.12.1 settings, retaining held-B closed-eye patterns and increasing screen ripple/lens distortion by 25% over 0.12.0. Audio and the other treatment settings remain unchanged.

Version 0.2.6 restores the polished six-model set and native Lit material bindings from the newer integration checkout. The registration, printing and cutting mechanics remain the same. The polished cabinet separates its door and working tray for animation, and its camera moves onto the cabinet's centerline, facing its front directly. The prototype generators and active model assets have been replaced. Build and runtime model contracts reject obsolete bundles instead of silently reusing them.

Version 0.2.7 adds closer cabinet framing and an overhead sheet-transfer camera. The bench has one edition selector, Draw, Create batch and Load dry sheet. Packaging requires a native station, and Esc directly leaves idle controls, drawing, cutting and cabinet interactions.

## Play the slice

Use the copied **fifth save slot**. Own and access the Sewer Office through the native game. Find the checkerboard Off Register sticker on a wall near the office entrance and select **Meet the collective**. Its prompt is anchored at the sticker's centre. This adds its message thread, opens the Studio phone catalogue and removes the sticker. The native **First edition** tutorial retains its eighteen separate purchase, placement and production objectives. Purchased supplies remain checked after use; saved progress from the old grouped tutorial maps onto the corresponding completed objectives. Existing introduced saves receive the tutorial too, with progress inferred from their equipment and stock.

1. Buy a **Blotter Bench ($350)**, **Blotter drying cabinet ($250)**, **Blank blotter stock ($4)** and **Empty edition sleeves ($1)** from **Oscar's warehouse store**. Buy **Print cartridges ($6)** at either hardware store.
2. Place the bench and cabinet inside the owned Sewer Office. Active machines cannot be picked up or destroyed until their output is collected.
3. Order **Prism Base ($30 per parcel)** through the Studio phone app. This precursor uses its wrapped-parcel icon, stored model and held model, and is marked **illegal contraband** in the native item definition. Payment uses the native online account. The opaque catalogue refreshes delivery status automatically, without balance or order-hash clutter. Follow the **purple star on the compass and map** to **Dan's Hardware delivery pallet** after thirty game minutes. The marker stays until pickup. A full pallet holds the order; it does not overwrite other deliveries or impose a pickup deadline.
4. Carry one parcel, one blank sheet and one cartridge to the bench. Use the single **Edition ▶** button to cycle through presets and saved designs, **Draw** to edit, **Create batch** to start production, or **Load dry sheet** to cut. The bench has no packaging shortcuts.
5. The camera moves onto the physical sheet. **Register** it by dragging the red mark onto the green cross and holding steady; arrow keys follow screen directions. **Print** by dragging the roller from the paper's lower screen edge to its upper edge, revealing the artwork behind it. The roller, handle and reveal use the same physical sheet coordinates. Space rolls steadily as an alternative. Leaving restores movement, camera and the selected hotbar item.
6. Collect the wet sheet and carry it to the cabinet. The camera moves closer in front of the cabinet, then above the fully extended tray for sheet placement or pickup. Choose **Load wet sheet**, then click the physical door handle, tray lip, sheet, tray lip and handle in turn: the door swings open, the tray slides out, the sheet moves onto it, the tray returns and the door closes. **Space** advances each action as an alternative. If carrying several wet editions, the selected hotbar sheet takes priority. Drying starts when the door closes, takes two game hours and continues while away or after sleeping. The visible sheet shifts from a cool wet tint to a warm dry tint; the lamp shows idle, drying or ready. **Collect dry sheet** uses the same door/tray sequence with the sheet lifted out. Esc before completion restores the saved contents; a full inventory leaves ready output in the cabinet. Wet inventory sheets have a strong grid, blue tint and water-drop badge; ready sheets have a warm border, grid and check badge. Load the ready sheet into the bench. **Cut** it on the physical cutter by following eleven separate guide lines, starting at each white cursor. The small knife and its handle travel together along the actual active line, including horizontal cuts. A grabbed stroke stays active through fast edge overshoot and samples the release position. Release and grab near the current cut point to resume it; straight-line overshoot does not reduce accuracy. Space advances one cut at a time as an alternative.
7. Collect **32–40 Standard-quality tabs**. Mistakes reduce yield, while the surviving tabs keep their quality and edition. Uncollected output remains in its machine when inventory is full.
8. Package matching loose tabs at a **native packaging station**: one tab uses a baggie, five use a jar, and twenty use an **Empty edition sleeve**. Put the sleeve in the station's packaging input and matching loose tabs in its product input, then pack and seal normally. Sleeves now have a native packaging definition and twenty alignment positions. Tabs and their physical colliders are **55% smaller**; filled bag/jar contents use the same scale. Store and sell the resulting native product as usual; the finished edition is listed when first collected. Old brick-packaged editions remain compatible.
9. Consume a tab to start the approved visual/audio treatment. Hold **B** during the treatment for closed-eye patterns.

In native packaging tasks, loose tabs have one solid, small collision body with a modest thickness for physics, continuous collision detection and stable rotation. Only LSD tasks use a shorter native package-contact interval so a released tab is accepted before it slips through. The tab still has to contact the package's native trigger and be released; ordinary product packaging retains its original settings.

Save drawings with **Save edition**. Production and drying use their normal controls and timing; the temporary test hotkeys have been removed.

## Artwork and saving

Three presets—Sunburn, Prism and Night Shift—have identical production opportunities. Draw directly onto the physical lightbox paper using eight colors, brush sizes, erasing, undo and a name. The underlying artwork is 64 × 64 pixels. Choose full design or repeated motif. Sheets display an eight-by-five perforation grid; a loose tab displays the full image or one complete repeated motif, with a small paper-shaped inventory icon. Saving creates an immutable edition: already printed stock keeps its original artwork. This slice allows five custom revisions per save. Reuse them across as many batches as desired.

A compact bottom control strip supplies edition selection, drawing tools and progress; the work itself is rendered on the physical sheet. Registration, roller printing and guided cutting have separate controls and physical feedback. Assistance, production queues, upgrades, additional formulas/material grades and business receiving remain outside this slice.

The edition sleeve currently uses the native baggie folding/sealing scaffold, with twenty tab positions and a finished sleeve model. Its filled inventory icon uses the native fallback; a custom envelope animation and further supply-art polish remain for a later pass.

Native inventory and placed equipment save normally. Workshop state is stored under the selected save's `Modded/OffRegister/Workshop.json`, with a previous-file copy after subsequent saves. It preserves introduction, tutorial progress, edition pixels, order identities, machine GUIDs/stages, yield and drying deadlines. The 0.1 save schema remains compatible. Loading a damaged or unsupported workshop file stops workshop initialization and preserves that file. Reopening an interrupted manual stage does not consume its supplies again. Partial minigame progress restarts; batches and finished output remain.

Keep **both personal LSD DLLs and matching S1API installed** while this save contains studio items. Back up the full save before removing the workshop. The slice uses eight stable native edition IDs across saves; custom names appear in the studio, while inventory names use Studio 01–05 for those revisions.

## Source and checks

Run `scripts/Build-LSDGameplay.ps1` to build the unified Substances module, run the workshop and treatment checks, and package its DLL with Core. Builds require `studio.contract.json` with matching bundle, model, icon and generator hashes. Missing or obsolete assets stop the build. `-RebuildArt` regenerates the polished Blender props, checks their geometry contracts, renders inventory icons and builds the Unity bundle using Blender 5.2 and Unity 2022.3.62f2. Editable Blender files, generators, PNGs, FBX files and the Unity project are retained in the repository. Geometry and icons are authored locally; models and paper use the installed game's native URP Lit shader, while interaction guides stay unlit.

The opt-in native diagnostic uses `--lsd-slice-check`, with `--lsd-slice-exercise` or `--lsd-slice-reload` and `--lsd-check-output=<directory>`. Exercise mode intentionally places equipment, changes inventory, skips drying and saves **only slot five**. Back up that slot first and restore it after the check. Ordinary launches never run this scenario or auto-load a save.

The focused `--lsd-sheet-check` mode uses an existing placed bench in the copied fifth slot to check screen-up roller motion, reveal placement, knife travel along all eleven cut lines, distinct native sheet icons, the precursor model, pointer projection beyond paper edges and hotbar restoration. It also checks held tabs are rejected and ordinary native packaging keeps its original contact interval. These modes do not explicitly save; preserve slot five before running them and restore it afterward.
