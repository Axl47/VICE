# Off Register workshop — first playable slice

Personal LSD Workshop 0.2.3 implements the starter manual loop from [the gameplay design](lsd-gameplay-design.md), with drawing and distinct minigames on the physical equipment. It requires the beta / IL2CPP game, MelonLoader 0.7.3 and S1API (Forked by Bars) 3.2.1-beta.7. The companion visual treatment is Personal LSD Preview 0.11.2; its approved 0.11 visual/audio parameters are unchanged.

## Play the slice

Use the copied **fifth save slot**. Own and access the Sewer Office through the native game. Find the checkerboard Off Register sticker on a wall near the office entrance and select **Meet the collective**. Its prompt is anchored at the sticker's centre. This adds its message thread, opens the Studio phone catalogue and removes the sticker. The native **First edition** tutorial guides purchases, setup, printing and packaging. Existing introduced saves receive the tutorial too, with progress inferred from their equipment and stock.

1. Buy a **Blotter Bench ($350)**, **Blotter drying cabinet ($250)**, **Blank blotter stock ($4)** and **Empty edition sleeves ($1)** from **Oscar's warehouse store**. Buy **Print cartridges ($6)** at either hardware store.
2. Place the bench and cabinet inside the owned Sewer Office. Active machines cannot be picked up or destroyed until their output is collected.
3. Order **Prism Base ($30 per parcel)** through the Studio phone app. This precursor uses its wrapped-parcel icon, stored model and held model, and is marked **illegal contraband** in the native item definition. Payment uses the native online account. The opaque catalogue refreshes delivery status automatically, without balance or order-hash clutter. Follow the **purple star on the compass and map** to **Dan's Hardware delivery pallet** after thirty game minutes. The marker stays until pickup. A full pallet holds the order; it does not overwrite other deliveries or impose a pickup deadline.
4. Carry one parcel, one blank sheet and one cartridge to the bench. Select a preset or a saved custom edition and start a batch.
5. The camera moves onto the physical sheet. **Register** it by dragging the red mark onto the green cross and holding steady; arrow keys follow screen directions. **Print** by dragging the roller from the paper's lower screen edge to its upper edge, revealing the artwork behind it. The roller, handle and reveal use the same physical sheet coordinates. Space rolls steadily as an alternative. Leaving restores movement, camera and the selected hotbar item.
6. Collect the wet sheet and carry it to the cabinet. Wet inventory sheets have a strong grid, blue tint and water-drop badge; ready sheets have a warm border, grid and check badge. Drying takes two game hours and continues while away or after sleeping. Its lamp shows idle, drying or ready. Collect the ready sheet and load it into the bench. **Cut** it on the physical cutter by following eleven separate guide lines, starting at each white cursor. The small knife and its handle travel together along the actual active line, including horizontal cuts. A grabbed stroke stays active through fast edge overshoot and samples the release position. Release and grab near the current cut point to resume it; straight-line overshoot does not reduce accuracy. Space advances one cut at a time as an alternative.
7. Collect **32–40 Standard-quality tabs**. Mistakes reduce yield, while the surviving tabs keep their quality and edition. Uncollected output remains in its machine when inventory is full.
8. Package matching loose tabs at the bench or at a **native packaging station**: one tab uses a baggie, five use a jar, and twenty use an **Empty edition sleeve**. Put the sleeve in the station's packaging input and matching loose tabs in its product input, then pack and seal normally. Sleeves now have a native packaging definition and twenty alignment positions. Tabs and their physical colliders are **55% smaller**; filled bag/jar contents use the same scale. Store and sell the resulting native product as usual; the finished edition is listed when first collected. Old brick-packaged editions remain compatible.
9. Consume a tab to start the approved visual/audio treatment. **F8** remains the manual preview/fade control. **F9** clears the treatment outside workshop controls.

In native packaging tasks, loose tabs have one solid, small collision body with a modest thickness for physics, continuous collision detection and stable rotation. Only LSD tasks use a shorter native package-contact interval so a released tab is accepted before it slips through. The tab still has to contact the package's native trigger and be released; ordinary product packaging retains its original settings.

**Temporary testing shortcut:** while workshop controls are open, **F9** saves the current drawing or finishes the current manual stage and every drying cabinet. When standing inside the Sewer Office with controls closed, F9 finishes drying cabinets. Collection still uses the normal inventory-capacity checks. This shortcut is intended for testing and can be removed after the slice settles.

## Artwork and saving

Three presets—Sunburn, Prism and Night Shift—have identical production opportunities. Draw directly onto the physical lightbox paper using eight colors, brush sizes, erasing, undo and a name. The underlying artwork is 64 × 64 pixels. Choose full design or repeated motif. Sheets display an eight-by-five perforation grid; a loose tab displays the full image or one complete repeated motif, with a small paper-shaped inventory icon. Saving creates an immutable edition: already printed stock keeps its original artwork. This slice allows five custom revisions per save. Reuse them across as many batches as desired.

A compact bottom control strip supplies edition selection, drawing tools and progress; the work itself is rendered on the physical sheet. Registration, roller printing and guided cutting have separate controls and physical feedback. Assistance, production queues, upgrades, additional formulas/material grades and business receiving remain outside this slice.

The edition sleeve currently uses the native baggie folding/sealing scaffold, with twenty tab positions and a finished sleeve model. Its filled inventory icon uses the native fallback; a custom envelope animation and further supply-art polish remain for a later pass.

Native inventory and placed equipment save normally. Workshop state is stored under the selected save's `Modded/OffRegister/Workshop.json`, with a previous-file copy after subsequent saves. It preserves introduction, tutorial progress, edition pixels, order identities, machine GUIDs/stages, yield and drying deadlines. The 0.1 save schema remains compatible. Loading a damaged or unsupported workshop file stops workshop initialization and preserves that file. Reopening an interrupted manual stage does not consume its supplies again. Partial minigame progress restarts; batches and finished output remain.

Keep **both personal LSD DLLs and matching S1API installed** while this save contains studio items. Back up the full save before removing the workshop. The slice uses eight stable native edition IDs across saves; custom names appear in the studio, while inventory names use Studio 01–05 for those revisions.

## Source and checks

Run `scripts/Build-LSDGameplay.ps1` to build both modules, run the workshop rule checks and the preview's timing/render/audio guards, and package the DLLs. `-RebuildArt` regenerates the authored Blender props, transparent inventory icons and Unity bundle using Blender 5.2 and Unity 2022.3.62f2. Editable Blender files are generated under `assets/LSDStudio/Blender`; the generators, rendered PNGs, FBX files and Unity project are retained in the repository. Geometry and icons are authored locally; the runtime uses the installed game's native URP shader and existing packaging/consumption behavior.

The opt-in native diagnostic uses `--lsd-slice-check`, with `--lsd-slice-exercise` or `--lsd-slice-reload` and `--lsd-check-output=<directory>`. Exercise mode intentionally places equipment, changes inventory, skips drying and saves **only slot five**. Back up that slot first and restore it after the check. Ordinary launches never run this scenario or auto-load a save.

The focused `--lsd-sheet-check` mode uses an existing placed bench in the copied fifth slot to check screen-up roller motion, reveal placement, knife travel along all eleven cut lines, distinct native sheet icons, the precursor model, pointer projection beyond paper edges and hotbar restoration. Adding `--lsd-packaging-check` places a diagnostic station and exercises real native physics contacts for five baggie tasks and two five-tab jar tasks, then checks sealing and output quantities. It also checks held tabs are rejected and ordinary native packaging keeps its original contact interval. These modes do not explicitly save; preserve slot five before running them and restore it afterward. The 0.2.2 checks passed; native logs, generated sheet-icon PNGs, build hashes and save-restoration proof are retained under `artifacts/lsd-0.2.2-check`. Pure cut-stroke checks cover fast overshoot, release-frame completion, mid-stroke resumption and accurate overshoot scoring.

Multiplayer uses host-authorized transactions, machine ownership, matching edition definitions and replicated artwork/state. A second-player playtest remains required before treating multiplayer as verified. Native customer handover and balance tuning also need manual playtesting alongside the automated quantity and save checks.

On October 7, the 0.2 native fifth-slot exercise passed introduction, opening and closing the native phone catalogue, in-world custom drawing and paper projection, distinct registration/roller/cutting tasks, hotbar restoration, paid ordering and physical pallet delivery, F9 drying completion, full-inventory rejection, tutorial completion, consumption and native saving. It also exercised native manual packaging tasks for baggies, jars and twenty-tab sleeves, including their product alignment, sealing and exact output quantities. A separate fresh launch passed restoration of placed equipment, package quantities, an interrupted batch, immutable pixels, tutorial progress, introduction and a pending order without duplicate delivery. Local evidence is retained under `artifacts/lsd-0.2-check` as `exercise.log`, `reload.log`, screenshots, expected snapshots and pass markers. The diagnostic save is preserved separately; the user's latest fifth-slot playtest save was restored with all 43 file hashes matching, and the original third slot also remained unchanged.
