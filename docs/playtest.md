# Live playtest

Target: Schedule I IL2CPP beta, MelonLoader 0.7.3. The original suite targeted 0.4.7f7; UI Enhancements 0.2.4 was built and audited against 0.4.7f10. Build/startup results are recorded separately in `validation.md`. Run these checks on a copied/test save.

1. Launch with the seven personal DLLs, S1API and TheRegulars. Confirm all seven load in `MelonLoader/Latest.log` without personal-mod patch/asset warnings. Warehouse must report 0.1.1; Better Police must report 0.2.0; Casino must report 0.1.2; More Guns must report 0.2.0; UI Enhancements must report 0.2.4. Reach the menu and load a save; see `startup-crash.md` if verifying the startup fix.
2. Visit the warehouse during the day and after midnight. Test the pedestrian entrance and roller doors. Check Oscar's small/medium/large racks, small/medium/large/huge storage closets, lockers and filing/display cabinets. Purchase/place an item; return to the menu and reload to check catalog persistence.
3. Start a police chase. Check near/far speed, detection to the sides, occlusion through a wall, arrest timing, aim and nearby backup. End pursuit and confirm normal movement speed returns.
4. Open an ordinary customer's counteroffer. Check the displayed estimate/budget and manually modify product, quantity and price. Deliver exact packages, then try insufficient/mixed packaging. Verify unmatched deliveries remain manual and inventory does not change when no exact plan exists. Confirm special customer deals from TheRegulars stay under that mod's control.
5. Open blackjack and Ride the Bus separately. Drag each bet slider in both directions, check that the amount label follows and confirm the maximum reaches $100,000 (or its configured limit). With enough cash, place and resolve a wager above $1,000; verify the displayed bet, cash deduction and win/loss payout. Select an unaffordable bet and confirm normal readiness validation prevents playing. Close/reopen both tables and retry after a save reload. Check the log has no `GetBetLimits` trampoline errors. Verify slot machines show their original wager choices and payouts after restarting with Casino 0.1.2; old `SlotBetMultiplier` config values must have no effect.
6. Buy both custom guns and matching magazines. Check icons, first/third-person models, automatic fire and ammo decrement. Empty the AK47, reload and confirm it reaches 30 rounds while consuming a spare; repeat for the minigun's 400 rounds. Try a partial reload and a reload without a spare. Collect discarded magazines and reload a save containing them; check for `TrashItem.Initialize` errors. Hold minigun aim without firing and watch the barrels speed up; release/re-aim before the delay ends, then reload/switch weapons while spinning. Firing must still wait for a fresh full spin-up and use its normal muzzle/reload animations. Compare both guns' tighter spread, reduced impact force and softer screen shake. Set effects volume to zero, restore it, then repeat with master volume; firing, reload and windup must follow the sliders. Switch repeatedly between the two guns, a spare magazine and an ordinary gun; confirm models remain equipped and ordinary gun/collision camera effects retain their normal behavior. Save/reload with a custom gun and confirm persistence.
7. At Gas-Mart Central, put several products in the cart, open **Saved orders**, enter a name and save the cart. Change the cart, select the saved list and load it; verify exact quantities and current prices, then check out normally. **Repeat last** must restore that completed purchase. Restart/reload and repeat at Gas-Mart West. Save the same name to update its quantities and delete a named list; last-purchase history should remain.
8. Test a saved gas-mart list with an unavailable/locked/insufficient-stock product: it must show the reason and preserve the existing cart. With too little money or inventory capacity, a valid list can load but normal checkout must block purchase. Failed checkout must not replace last-purchase history. Close the shop while typing an order name and verify game keyboard input resumes. Open the hardware/warehouse stores and confirm no gas-mart buttons appear there.
9. Accept and track two customer deals with different agreed payments and quantities. Check the total and one-decimal unit value beside each title in the left HUD list against the accepted terms: $1,000 for 30 units should show $33.3/unit. Advance time, untrack/retrack and complete a deal: timing/objectives must remain readable, amounts must not duplicate, and finished rows must disappear normally. Try a long name/large payment, multiple products if available and an ordinary quest; ordinary quests must keep their original labels. Test a special customer's contract if it uses the standard tracked deal UI.
10. Open the map after unlocking several customers. Check portraits at the customers' current positions, hover names, click-to-focus, controller navigation, panning and zooming. Follow one moving customer, then a customer entering/leaving a building; the indoor pin must use its entrance. A potential/locked customer must retain its original search zone and receive no new portrait. Unlock a new customer and reopen the map; confirm its pin appears once. Close/reopen the phone/map several times, return to the menu and load a different save; pins must not duplicate or persist from the previous save. Check inactive/dead customers are omitted and available known custom NPCs work without UI warnings.
11. Restart with `ShowDealPayouts=false`, then with `ShowKnownCustomers=false`, and verify each addition can be disabled independently. Test the configured icon scale. Removing only `Personal.UIEnhancements.dll` must restore native labels/map presentation without a save migration.
12. For multiplayer, repeat weapon/wager checks with matching DLLs/config on every peer. UI Enhancements and gas-mart lists are local to each player's computer; check customer pin positions on a joining client too. Multiplayer is not established by a solo startup check.
13. With Better Police 0.2.0, start a foot pursuit with two officers. Run across open streets, then hold position in a building with two reachable entrances: one officer should keep pressure while another approaches from the front or other entrance. Try a single-entrance corridor; supporting officers should hold spaced positions instead of inventing a route through a wall. Let a flanker complete its route and verify it can close in to arrest/fight. Kill or disable the lead officer, lose all line of sight for over six seconds, and end the pursuit. Check roles recover, native searching continues and normal movement resumes. Try a changing door/vehicle obstruction and verify officers give up stalled waypoints. Repeat as host with a joining client using matching settings; investigate any police route warnings.
14. In a hostile-cartel save with eligible regions, wait for the current loaded countdown to finish. Compare subsequent activity cooldowns and player ambushes near configured zones with `RivalGang=true` versus `false`; default new waits should be approximately half as long (rounded up) and detection areas 50% wider. Confirm native goon counts/weapons/pool limits, no gang ambush during police pursuit, and native behavior while truced or defeated. Change saves/restart and check there are no gang cooldown/ambush/radius-restore warnings. Do not use debug-triggered encounters to judge the scheduled frequency.
15. In multiplayer, have another player join and move around the host, then repeat on a joining client with UI Enhancements 0.2.4. Check one blue diamond per other player with the correct name in the same row as native distance labels, without top cropping; player markers show names instead of numbers, and your own player must have no new marker. Rotate, look behind, enter vehicles/buildings and use screens that hide the compass. Native direction/visibility rules must still apply. Have peers disconnect/rejoin and return to the menu/load another save: no stale or duplicate markers or compass warnings. Check long names truncate with an ellipsis, several nearby peers and HUD scaling. Normal quest/mission markers must retain their distance numbers. Restart with `ShowMultiplayerPlayers=false` and confirm only this addition disappears.
16. Load a save without first opening the phone. Check a bordered square at the top right, with map artwork, a centered player indicator and nearby native icons/known-customer portraits. Walk north/south/east/west, drive and teleport: the map must follow immediately while the player stays centered and north stays up. Compare nearby road intersections, shops, quest pins and potential-customer zones against the phone map; verify customer portraits still use indoor entrances and only eligible known customers. At the map edge, artwork/icons must clip inside the square. Watch zones whose centers lie outside but whose areas overlap the minimap.
17. Open the phone/map, pan and zoom, close it and move: the minimap must hide during phone use, return afterward and remain centered at its configured zoom. Repeat at least ten times using the phone hotkey, back/escape, app switching and controller close, including rapid reopen/close. No second map opening should be required to restore it, and its artwork must survive the hidden phone's fade/zero scale. The full map's controls, click-to-focus, player focus, markers and tooltips must work normally. Pause/resume and hide/show the HUD; minimap visibility must follow. Test different resolutions/aspect ratios and HUD scales for a square viewport, edge margin, legible icons and no blocked mouse/controller input.
18. Restart with `MinimapSize=160`, then `480`; repeat with `MinimapRange=60`, then `500`. Smaller range should show less world area at greater zoom, and changing size alone must preserve world coverage. Invalid numeric settings should fall back/clamp without warnings or blank output. Set `ShowMinimap=false`: no minimap should appear, and pricing/map portraits/compass markers must remain available. Disable `ShowKnownCustomers` independently: native minimap POIs should remain, with no added customer portraits. Return to the menu/load another save, including tutorial if available, and repeat as a multiplayer joining client; there must be one minimap, correct map artwork and no stale icons or `ui:minimap` warnings.

19. With UI Enhancements 0.2.4, compare local and remote player glyphs against the full map: foreground, outline/fill and native colors must appear instead of a black disc. Repeat as host and joining client, after respawn and disconnect/rejoin. At `MinimapIconScale=1.55`, compact icons should be 55% larger than at `1`; the local and remote player symbols should scale together, while large search zones retain their geographical footprint. Walk beyond the left/top/right water edges and verify the uncovered square matches the map's water color without a blue seam. Repeat on the tutorial map if available for its own border tint. Check map type/save changes restore the correct fill and no icon/border resources persist after unloading.

Report the failed step and relevant `MelonLoader/Latest.log` lines. Settings are in `UserData/MelonPreferences.cfg`; restart after changes.

20. With UI Enhancements 0.2.4, stand still and turn through north/east/south/west: the center glyph must point with the camera while the map stays north-up. Look sharply up/down and strafe/backpedal: direction should follow looking rather than travel, with no flipped/jumping icon at vertical pitch. Repeat before opening the phone, after repeated close/reopen, while driving, after respawn and as host/joining client. Compare the directional inner layers with the full map and check other players turn with their replicated POI direction. Original glyph colors, 55% enlargement, centering and non-player POI orientation must remain correct.

21. With UI Enhancements 0.2.4, turn slowly through a full 360 degrees and stop at cardinal and diagonal directions. The local player's circle, fill, outline, shadow and arrow must remain complete and centered, with no changing white/black arcs or cut-off portions. Compare with the phone map before and after opening/closing it and at different HUD scales/minimap zooms. Repeat for a nearby turning multiplayer peer and as a joining client. The map stays north-up; the circular layers and shadow stay fixed, and only the facing arrow turns. Native hover decoration with zero scale should remain hidden rather than retain stale geometry.

22. With UI Enhancements 0.2.4, stand still and sweep the camera very slowly through 360 degrees in approximately 1–2-degree increments. The white ring must retain the same shape and width instead of alternating between visible and missing segments. Repeat at HUD scales/resolutions and minimap range/size extremes, after opening/closing the phone, and for a turning multiplayer peer. The arrow must still orbit the dot and point at the current heading without turning the circle or shade.
