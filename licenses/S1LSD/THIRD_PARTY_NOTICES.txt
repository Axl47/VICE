THIRD-PARTY NOTICES
===================

The S1LSD release archive contains only S1LSD's own files (Mods/S1LSD.dll and a preset
JSON). It does not bundle any third-party library. The following projects are required
or referenced at runtime and are installed separately by the player; each is distributed
under its own license, available in its repository:

- MelonLoader (mod loader)       https://github.com/LavaGang/MelonLoader
- Il2CppInterop (IL2CPP bridge, shipped with MelonLoader)
                                 https://github.com/BepInEx/Il2CppInterop
- HarmonyX (patching, shipped with MelonLoader)
                                 https://github.com/BepInEx/HarmonyX
- Newtonsoft.Json (JSON, shipped with MelonLoader)
                                 https://github.com/JamesNK/Newtonsoft.Json
- S1API (Schedule I modding API) https://github.com/ifBars/S1API

The unit tests additionally use xUnit and Microsoft.NET.Test.Sdk from NuGet; they are
not part of the release.

Original content
----------------
All visual logic, the procedural icons (BlotterArt), the procedural audio
(ProceduralSynth) and the closed-eye pattern textures are generated by S1LSD's own code.
No game assets, third-party audio, images or shader code are copied or redistributed.
"Schedule I" is a trademark of its developer; S1LSD is an unofficial fan modification.
